    Learned visual fingerprints

    Official Community Forensics ViT-S/384, CLIP-normalized, WebGPU first.

    Generator traces

    PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF bytes are scanned for prompts, workflows, and generator names.

  3. 03
    Provenance clues

    C2PA, Content Credentials, SynthID labels, and known watermark markers — surfaced, not “decoded.”
